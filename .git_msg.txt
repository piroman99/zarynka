vn2: silence through the transition — the new scene's voice waits for the light

- enterPos used to start the page voice the moment the scene swapped in the dark
  (0.52 s into the fade), so the cabinet narration read UNDER the badge and there
  was almost no pause (Ivan: «пауза между прологом и кабинетом не тормозит голос,
  почти нет тишины»)
- sceneTrans now stops the voice up front and defers the new one: playVoice turns
  voDelay into a timer, so speech starts when the picture is light again plus
  TR_SILENCE (260 ms) — about 2.7 s of real silence from the click
- playVoice reads voDelay BEFORE stopVoice (which zeroes it); scheduleAuto returns
  early while voWaitMs > 0 and is re-armed by the deferred start, so auto mode
  never runs ahead of the voice
- a click mid-transition now also works after the scene has already swapped in the
  dark: transActive/endTrans end the fade and start the deferred voice at once
  (before, finishBreak silently did nothing past the 520 ms mark)
- new check _trans_voice_gap.py: voice starts after the lightening (2.68 s vs 1.70),
  click-skip, and auto mode — 10/10 green; _vn2_vo_break.py 14/14,
  _vn2_restart.py 3/3 (154 clicks)
