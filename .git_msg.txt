story: whole-story cast script (SCRIPT_CAST.md) + shared text segmenter

- SCRIPT_CAST.md: every story line -> who reads it -> text, for all 10 episodes
  (267 segments, 150 speech lines), generated by _story_cast.py from
  assets/story.txt verbatim; speakers: АВТОР, СТАРИК, СЕРГЕЙ, ЕКАТЕРИНА plus
  МУСАБАЕВ, ЛЁША (Савушкин, «поэт»), ТОЦКИЙ and САВУШКИН (epilogue preface)
- the cast map is declarative (line -> speakers of the speech segments in order);
  the script fails loudly when the map and the text disagree, so the markup
  cannot silently drift
- a chars-per-speaker summary doubles as the voice-over budget for the remaining
  episodes: narrator 17202, Старик 7762, Сергей 5135, Екатерина 2841,
  Тоцкий 1647, Мусабаев 686, Лёша 610
- _story_cast_check.py: ep-1 speaker order matches the built novel exactly (15
  speaker changes)
- segmentation moved to _text_seg.py, shared by the build and the script, and one
  rule fixed there: a dash after a COMMA is dialogue only when the paragraph
  already has a dialogue line — otherwise it is an in-sentence dash (story line
  321 was being read by a character). content.js of ep-1 is byte-identical after
  the change.
